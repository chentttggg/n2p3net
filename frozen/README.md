# Research version archives

Retain history by substantive architecture milestone, with one recovery manifest
for each major version. Its contents identify the actual backend and BrainSync
interfaces, necessary source/environment/model inputs, final accepted results
and the producers those results used. Verify member hashes and recovery before
retiring superseded material. A source snapshot or EXE launch does not prove an
integrated training release or adult performance.

Retirement follows the engineering guide's
[delivery and old-build cleanup procedure](../doc/engineering.zh.md#交付与旧构建清理).
Retain substantive recovery versions and unique evidence; use the cleanup receipt
to identify redundant generated copies and record their actual removal.

Original acquisitions, unique scientific inputs, final models/predictions/results,
credentials and other authors' WIP remain separate from disposable process files.
Archive payloads, materialized checkouts and raw data stay local; Git distributes
source, active guides and compact manifests. Obtain a referenced payload from its
custodian and verify its SHA-256 before use. Preserve actual source bytes and
producer identities; historical commit mappings stay local and are not rewritten
to match publication.

Start with the [current guides](../doc/README.md) and [evidence index](../doc/evidence/README.md).

Historical relative paths may identify local payloads or retired stage layouts.
Use the [document locator inventory](../doc/assets/repository_audit_20261019/docs_historical_index.json)
to distinguish a hash-verified original, a local-only payload and an original
target that has not been found. A replacement source archive does not recreate
the acceptance evidence of a missing historical manifest.

New public exports use relative paths or artifact keys and a separate summary
identity; original logs and manifests retain their bytes. Third-party code,
papers and datasets follow the [source and permission inventory](../doc/third_party_sources.json).
Raw materials and PDF copies are not included in a new source/evidence export
until redistribution rights for the exact copy have been verified. This does not
delete local evidence, choose a repository license or rewrite Git history.
